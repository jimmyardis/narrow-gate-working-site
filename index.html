About Narrow Gate

A journal of places, ideas
and the pursuit of beauty.

Narrow Gate brings together the work and interests of Vince Graham: a developer, civic artist, and independent thinker whose projects range from traditional neighborhoods to historic restoration.

This is a place for the long view. For essays and conversations, drawings and documents, unexpected connections, and the details that make a place worth caring about.

Beauty is the thread. The invitation is to look more closely—and to imagine what might yet be.

Sources & credits

Article titles, dates, and source links are drawn from The I’On Company, Loci, CNU, Strong Towns, Yeoman, Common Edge, Furman University, and the I’On Community archive. Short descriptions are editorial summaries.

Each entry identifies the author or publication and its format. Related reading and reported profiles are distinguished from Vince’s own writing. Dates on historical works may refer to the original work; source pages can show a later republication date. Project dates follow Loci’s archive.

Project and archive photographs come from Loci and the linked original publishers. The hero is an AI-created illustrative collage based on the supplied design, not a documentary photograph. Paper texture is generated; collection engravings are preserved from the supplied mockup.

Sequence as Canon is the supplied manuscript, with publication status unconfirmed. The accompanying download is provided for project review.

This edition is a curated collection, not a claim to a complete bibliography. Some original sources may change or require access through their publisher.
